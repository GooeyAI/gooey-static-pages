How Gemini 3.1 Pro compares in Kinyarwanda

Gemini 3.1 Pro is above average in Kinyarwanda accuracy (score 90, #2 of 5 models) but among the slowest: it answers in 8.9 seconds, #5 of 5 and inside the 10-second limit for phone calls. Every model was charged the same, about $40.00 per 1,000 questions. It cannot be self-hosted. The best self-hostable chain, DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual, scores 92, 2 points higher.

Frequently asked questions

How good is Gemini 3.1 Pro at Kinyarwanda?

As of August 2026, Gemini 3.1 Pro listening to the audio directly scores 90 out of 100 on the Kinyarwanda questions, #2 of 5 models. It answers in 8.9 seconds.

Does Gemini 3.1 Pro understand Kinyarwanda?

Partly. Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 90 out of 100 on spoken Kinyarwanda questions with built-in audio, #2 of 5 models; DeepSeek V4 scored 92.

Can I run Gemini 3.1 Pro on my own servers?

No. Gemini 3.1 Pro is only available from Google’s cloud. The best chain you can host yourself is DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual (score 92).

What does Gemini 3.1 Pro cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for Gemini 3.1 Pro + built-in audio was $40.00 per 1,000 questions. Its carbon footprint is not covered by our EcoCost estimate yet.

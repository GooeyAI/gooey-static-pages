What to know about Gemini 3.1 Pro in Hausa

Gemini 3.1 Pro is the strongest model we tested for Hausa: it scores 71 on the Hausa Index, the highest of 6 models, against a median of 56. It listens to the audio directly, and answers in 16.3 seconds, faster than the median of 18.4 s and over the 10-second limit for phone calls.

Its carbon footprint is not covered by EcoCost for this chain yet. Pricing is coming soon.

It is proprietary and runs only in Google’s cloud.

Can I run Gemini 3.1 Pro on my own servers?

No. Gemini 3.1 Pro is proprietary and runs in Google’s cloud.

How are these results measured?

Each model answers the same spoken questions (30 in Hausa, 24 in Kikuyu, 24 in Kinyarwanda and 24 in Swahili). Each answer is graded against an expert’s answer, and the Index is the average match, from 0 to 100. We use each model’s best speech-recognition chain per language and update the page when we re-run the evaluation.

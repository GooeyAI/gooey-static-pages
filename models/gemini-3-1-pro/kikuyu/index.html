How Gemini 3.1 Pro compares in Kikuyu

Gemini 3.1 Pro is above average in Kikuyu accuracy (score 86, #1 of 6 models) but among the slowest: it answers in 9.5 seconds, #6 of 6 and inside the 10-second limit for phone calls. It is cheap at about $40.00 per 1,000 questions, the lowest of the 6. It cannot be self-hosted. The best self-hostable chain, DeepSeek V4 + Kikuyu Whisper v2, scores 60, 26 points lower.

Frequently asked questions

How good is Gemini 3.1 Pro at Kikuyu?

As of August 2026, Gemini 3.1 Pro with Kikuyu Whisper v2 speech recognition scores 86 out of 100 on the Kikuyu questions, #1 of 6 models. It answers in 9.5 seconds.

Does Gemini 3.1 Pro understand Kikuyu?

Partly. Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 86 out of 100 on spoken Kikuyu questions with Kikuyu Whisper v2, #1 of 6 models; Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 86.

Can I run Gemini 3.1 Pro on my own servers?

No. Gemini 3.1 Pro is only available from Google’s cloud. The best chain you can host yourself is DeepSeek V4 + Kikuyu Whisper v2 (score 60).

What does Gemini 3.1 Pro cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for Gemini 3.1 Pro + Kikuyu Whisper v2 was $40.00 per 1,000 questions. Its estimated footprint is 20.6 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions for the language-model step (confidence: low).

How often are these results updated?

The numbers on this page come from the Aug 30, 2026 run of 24 questions. We update the page when we re-run the benchmark.

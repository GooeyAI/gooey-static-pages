What to know about GPT-5.6 Sol in Hausa

GPT-5.6 Sol ranks #3 of 6 models for Hausa: it scores 59 on the Hausa Index, against a median of 56; Gemini 3.1 Pro leads with 71. It scores best with Omnilingual speech recognition, and answers in 14.4 seconds, faster than the median of 18.4 s and over the 10-second limit for phone calls.

Its language-model step emits about 388 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions, higher than the median of 355 g. Pricing is coming soon.

How are these results measured?

Each model answers the same spoken questions (30 in Hausa, 24 in Kikuyu, 24 in Kinyarwanda and 24 in Swahili). Each answer is graded against an expert’s answer, and the Index is the average match, from 0 to 100. We use each model’s best speech-recognition chain per language and update the page when we re-run the evaluation.

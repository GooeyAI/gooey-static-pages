How GPT-5.6 Sol compares in Hausa

GPT-5.6 Sol is above average in Hausa accuracy (score 59, #3 of 6 models) and among the fastest: it answers in 14.4 seconds, #2 of 6 and over the 10-second limit for phone calls. It is expensive at about $62.67 per 1,000 questions, the highest of the 6. It cannot be self-hosted. The best self-hostable chain, Kimi K3 + Omnilingual, scores 63, 4 points higher.

Frequently asked questions

How good is GPT-5.6 Sol at Hausa?

As of August 2026, GPT-5.6 Sol with Omnilingual speech recognition scores 59 out of 100 on the Hausa questions, #3 of 6 models. It answers in 14.4 seconds.

Does ChatGPT understand Hausa?

ChatGPT is OpenAI’s app; we test its models directly. GPT-5.6 Sol scored 59 on spoken Hausa questions, #3 of 6.

Can I run GPT-5.6 Sol on my own servers?

No. GPT-5.6 Sol is only available from OpenAI’s cloud. The best chain you can host yourself is Kimi K3 + Omnilingual (score 63).

What does GPT-5.6 Sol cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for GPT-5.6 Sol + Omnilingual was $62.67 per 1,000 questions. Its estimated footprint is 387.6 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions for the language-model step (confidence: low).

How often are these results updated?

The numbers on this page come from the Aug 30, 2026 run of 30 questions. We update the page when we re-run the benchmark.

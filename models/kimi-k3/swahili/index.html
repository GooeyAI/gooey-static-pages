Frequently asked questions

How good is Kimi K3 at Swahili?

As of August 2026, Kimi K3 with GPT Transcribe speech recognition scores 70 out of 100 on the Swahili questions, #6 of 6 models. It answers in 6.7 seconds.

Does Kimi K3 understand Swahili?

Partly. Kimi K3 scored 70 out of 100 on spoken Swahili questions with GPT Transcribe, #6 of 6 models; Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 95.

Can I run Kimi K3 on my own servers?

Yes. Kimi K3 has open weights, so you can host it yourself, together with an open speech model such as Omnilingual.

What does Kimi K3 cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for Kimi K3 + GPT Transcribe was $40.00 per 1,000 questions. Its carbon footprint is not covered by our EcoCost estimate yet.

How often are these results updated?

The numbers on this page come from the Aug 30, 2026 run of 24 questions. We update the page when we re-run the benchmark.

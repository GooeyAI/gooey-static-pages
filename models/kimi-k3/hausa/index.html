How Kimi K3 compares in Hausa

Frequently asked questions

How good is Kimi K3 at Hausa?

As of August 2026, Kimi K3 with Omnilingual speech recognition scores 63 out of 100 on the Hausa questions, #2 of 6 models. It answers in 20.8 seconds.

Does Kimi K3 understand Hausa?

Partly. Kimi K3 scored 63 out of 100 on spoken Hausa questions with Omnilingual, #2 of 6 models; Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 71.

Can I run Kimi K3 on my own servers?

Yes. Kimi K3 has open weights, so you can host it yourself, together with an open speech model such as Omnilingual.

What does Kimi K3 cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for Kimi K3 + Omnilingual was $40.00 per 1,000 questions. Its carbon footprint is not covered by our EcoCost estimate yet.

How often are these results updated?

The numbers on this page come from the Aug 30, 2026 run of 30 questions. We update the page when we re-run the benchmark.

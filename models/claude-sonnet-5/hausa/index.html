How Claude Sonnet 5 compares in Hausa

Claude Sonnet 5 is below average in Hausa accuracy (score 50, #5 of 6 models) but among the fastest: it answers in 13.6 seconds, #1 of 6 and over the 10-second limit for phone calls. Frequently asked questions

How good is Claude Sonnet 5 at Hausa?

As of August 2026, Claude Sonnet 5 with Omnilingual speech recognition scores 50 out of 100 on the Hausa questions, #5 of 6 models. It answers in 13.6 seconds.

Does Claude Sonnet 5 understand Hausa?

Partly. Claude Sonnet 5 scored 50 out of 100 on spoken Hausa questions with Omnilingual, #5 of 6 models; Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 71.

Can I run Claude Sonnet 5 on my own servers?

No. Claude Sonnet 5 is only available from Anthropic’s cloud. The best chain you can host yourself is Kimi K3 + Omnilingual (score 63).

What does Claude Sonnet 5 cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for Claude Sonnet 5 + Omnilingual was $50.00 per 1,000 questions. Its estimated footprint is 228.7 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions for the language-model step (confidence: low).

How often are these results updated?

The numbers on this page come from the Aug 30, 2026 run of 30 questions. We update the page when we re-run the benchmark.

What to know about Gemini 3.7 Flash

Gemini 3.7 Flash ranks #4 of 6 models for Hausa: it scores 54 on the Hausa Index, against a median of 56; Gemini 3.1 Pro leads with 71. It scores best with Intron speech recognition, and answers in 20.4 seconds, slower than the median of 18.4 s and over the 10-second limit for phone calls.

Its language-model step emits about 512 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions, higher than the median of 355 g. Pricing is coming soon.

It is proprietary and runs only in Google’s cloud.

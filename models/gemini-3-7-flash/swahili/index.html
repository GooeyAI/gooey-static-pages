How Gemini 3.7 Flash compares in Swahili

Gemini 3.7 Flash is about average in Swahili accuracy (score 88, #5 of 6 models) and among the slowest: it answers in 7.1 seconds, #5 of 6 and well inside the 10-second limit for phone calls. Every model was charged the same, about $40.00 per 1,000 questions. It cannot be self-hosted. The best self-hostable chain, DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual, scores 93, 5 points higher.

Frequently asked questions

How good is Gemini 3.7 Flash at Swahili?

As of August 2026, Gemini 3.7 Flash with GPT Transcribe speech recognition scores 88 out of 100 on the Swahili questions, #5 of 6 models. It answers in 7.1 seconds.

Does Gemini 3.7 Flash understand Swahili?

Partly. Gemini 3.7 Flash scored 88 out of 100 on spoken Swahili questions with GPT Transcribe, #5 of 6 models; Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 95.

Can I run Gemini 3.7 Flash on my own servers?

No. Gemini 3.7 Flash is only available from Google’s cloud. The best chain you can host yourself is DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual (score 93).

What does Gemini 3.7 Flash cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge for Gemini 3.7 Flash + GPT Transcribe was $40.00 per 1,000 questions. Its estimated footprint is 53.9 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions for the language-model step (confidence: low).

How often are these results updated?

The numbers on this page come from the Aug 30, 2026 run of 24 questions. We update the page when we re-run the benchmark.

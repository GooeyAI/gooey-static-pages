Frequently asked questions

How good is DeepSeek V4 at Hausa?

As of August 2026, DeepSeek V4 with Omnilingual speech recognition scores 47 out of 100 on the Hausa questions, #6 of 6 models. It answers in 29.6 seconds.

Does DeepSeek V4 understand Hausa?

Partly. DeepSeek V4 scored 47 out of 100 on spoken Hausa questions with Omnilingual, #6 of 6 models; Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 71.

Can I run DeepSeek V4 on my own servers?

Yes. DeepSeek V4 has open weights, so you can host it yourself, together with an open speech model such as Omnilingual.

What does DeepSeek V4 cost, and what is its carbon footprint?

Pricing is coming soon. Its estimated footprint is 322.0 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions for the language-model step (confidence: low).

What to know about DeepSeek V4

DeepSeek V4 ranks #6 of 6 models for Hausa: it scores 47 on the Hausa Index, against a median of 56; Gemini 3.1 Pro leads with 71. It scores best with Omnilingual speech recognition, and answers in 29.6 seconds, slower than the median of 18.4 s and over the 10-second limit for phone calls.

Its language-model step emits about 322 g CO₂e per 1,000 questions, lower than the median of 355 g. Pricing is coming soon.

Its weights are open, so you can run it on your own servers.

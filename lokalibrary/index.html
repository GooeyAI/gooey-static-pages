Overview

Karnataka’s 5,000+ librarians serve as knowledge mediators between diverse communities and digital information ecosystems, from village panchayat libraries to city public archives. As AI reshapes access to information, this group stands at the frontline of digital inclusion — yet most lack a conceptual or practical understanding of AI and generative AI tools.

Our vision

This initiative uniquely positions Karnataka’s librarians as cultural data stewards in the age of AI, bridging public knowledge, technology, and community identity. Unlike generic digital literacy efforts, it fuses AI toolmaking, open-data ethics, and cultural participation into one integrated framework. Delivered bilingually through Gooey.AI’s participatory workflows, the program empowers librarians to not only understand AI but to shape it — curating datasets that reflect Karnataka’s languages, imagery, and heritage.

The vision is to transform libraries into living laboratories of inclusive innovation, where citizens encounter AI as something they can question, train, and trust. By teaching AI to see Karnataka through its libraries, the project models how public institutions can lead in building ethical, locally grounded, and globally resonant AI ecosystems — a blueprint for participatory AI emerging from the Global South.

Working with librarians as co-designers, we will develop community-led bird field guides that document local bird species through naming, observation, description, and collective knowledge gathering. Each participating library will contribute pages to a community-fuelled guide of birds across Karnataka — a rich, plural, and regionally grounded record of avian life, and an alternative to conventionally colonial approaches to natural history documentation.

Building on these field guides, librarians and their communities will explore how birds live within local stories, literature, and folk art traditions — reimagining species through local narratives, legends, and heroes, and bringing them to life as short looped video clips interweaving folk representations with observations and footage of the birds themselves.

Timeline & outputs

A 3-month pilot program with 31 librarian fellows across districts — combining online AI foundations, workshops, hands-on assignments, and the development of the Beyond Bias AI Library of Commons.

# Model page copy

Every piece of text on the generated `/models/<model>/<language>/` pages, in page order, and how each one is built.
The pages are generated by `scripts/build_model_pages.py` from `model.tmpl` and `model-changed.tmpl` (this folder);
sample-card labels are in `language/assets/language-samples.js`. Change the wording there, not in the HTML.

Kinds: **Fixed** (same on every page) · **Fill-in** (a sentence with `{blanks}`) · **Rule** (the builder picks a phrasing
from the numbers; alternatives are separated by `|`) · **Data** (shown as written in a data file).

Examples are from GPT-5.6 Sol · Kinyarwanda. "Versions" counts the different texts across the 23 pages as built on
20 Nov 2026; an interactive version with every page's text is the "Model Page Copy Inventory" artifact.

```text

```

### Paragraph · Rule · 23 versions

```text
{Model} is {level} in {Lang} accuracy (score {score}, #{rank} of {N} models) {and|but} {speed}: it answers in {time} seconds, #{speed rank} of {N}{phone}. {hosting sentence}
```
- level: “above average” / “about average” / “below average” (within 1 point of the mean of the N best scores = about).
- speed: top third by time “among the fastest”, bottom third “among the slowest”, else “mid-table on speed”.
- and/but: “but” when accuracy and speed point opposite ways.
- phone: ≤ 8 s “and well inside the 10-second limit for phone calls”; < 10 s “and inside…”; else “and over the 10-second limit…”.
- hosting: open weights “It can be self-hosted: its weights are open.”; else “It cannot be self-hosted. The best self-hostable chain, {chain}, scores {s}, {d} points higher/lower.”; or “…, and no chain we tested can.”

Example: GPT-5.6 Sol is below average in Kinyarwanda accuracy (score 85, #4 of 5 models) but among the fastest: it answers in 6.9 seconds, #2 of 5 and well inside the 10-second limit for phone calls. It cannot be self-hosted. The best self-hostable chain, DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual, scores 92, 7 points higher.

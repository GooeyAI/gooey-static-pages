      Frequently asked questions

      What is the best AI for Hausa?

      Gemini 3.1 Pro (direct audio) was the highest-scoring chain in our 2026-08-30 Hausa benchmark. It achieved a Hausa Index of 71/100 with a median response time of 16.3 seconds across 30 questions; see the benchmark results. This result applies to the tested questions and workflows, not every use case.

      Can AI models understand and answer questions in Hausa?

      Yes—the tested workflows processed spoken Hausa questions and produced answers in English. Across 13 chains, the Hausa Index ranged from 38 to 71/100 in the benchmark results. These are complete-workflow results, not standalone speech-recognition scores or a test of answers written in Hausa.

      Can I run Hausa AI on my own servers?

      Open-weight language models such as DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3 offer a starting point for running Hausa AI on your own servers. A fully local voice workflow also requires speech recognition that supports Hausa on local hardware. Choose hardware that meets the models’ requirements and check each component’s licence and language support before deployment.

      Which Hausa AI models are best for phone calls and WhatsApp?

      None of the 13 tested Hausa chains met our 10-second median response-time target for phone calls; the fastest was Claude Sonnet 5 + Omnilingual at 13.6 seconds. For WhatsApp voice-note replies, Gemini 3.1 Pro (direct audio) had the highest Hausa Index (71/100) in the benchmark results. These timings exclude a complete phone connection and spoken reply; test telephony, network and text-to-speech latency before launch.

      What does the Hausa Index measure?

      The Hausa Index is the mean semantic-similarity score between a workflow’s answers and the reference answers, scaled from 0 to 100. The evaluation CSV records scores for the 30 benchmark questions, with a higher Index indicating closer agreement in meaning. It is not the percentage of questions answered correctly, a standalone transcription score or a safety certification.

      How were the Hausa AI models evaluated?

      On 2026-08-30, we evaluated 13 chains using 30 spoken Hausa questions: questions about pregnancy, childbirth and newborn care. The workflows used direct audio or speech recognition with a language model, and their answers were scored by semantic similarity against reference answers drawn from Essential obstetric and newborn care; the evaluation CSV contains the questions, references, scores and run links. Scores are averaged and response times are medians; this small, health-focused dataset does not establish performance across all clinical scenarios, accents or real-world conditions. These benchmark results are not a clinical safety assessment. Any clinical or patient-facing use requires qualified clinical review.

      What is the environmental impact of Hausa AI models?

      Our EcoCost estimates cover text-model inference for 10 of the 13 tested Hausa chains, not their full environmental impact. The EcoCost snapshot reports estimated CO₂e per 1,000 questions and uncertainty ranges with low confidence. Speech recognition and direct-audio inference are excluded, and missing estimates are not zero; these data cannot establish the lowest-impact complete voice workflow.

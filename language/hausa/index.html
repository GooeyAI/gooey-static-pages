      Frequently asked questions

      Can I run a Hausa AI on my own servers?

      Four chains can: Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4, each with Omnilingual or Intron speech recognition. Both models have open weights, Omnilingual is open, and Intron says Sahara v2 can run offline on local hardware. Kimi K3 with Omnilingual also scored highest of the four in our Aug 30, 2026 test (63 out of 100, 20.8 s). Every other chain needs a private API, which sends audio to its maker’s cloud.

      What is the best AI for Hausa?

      In our Aug 30, 2026 test, Gemini 3.1 Pro, which listens to the audio directly, scored highest (71 out of 100, 16.3 s). Kimi K3 with Omnilingual came next (63), then GPT-5.6 Sol with Omnilingual (59). This benchmark uses health questions, so scores are not directly comparable with the other language pages.

      Which is best for phone calls, and which for WhatsApp?

      No chain answered within the 10 s limit for live calls: the fastest is Claude Sonnet 5 with Omnilingual at 13.6 s, but it scored 50. The fastest chain scoring 51 or more is GPT-5.6 Sol with Omnilingual (14.4 s, score 59), and the fastest sovereign-deployable one is Kimi K3 with Omnilingual (20.8 s, score 63). For WhatsApp voice notes and text a few seconds matter less: Gemini 3.1 Pro with direct audio is the most accurate (71), and Kimi K3 with Omnilingual is the best sovereign-deployable chain (63).

      Does ChatGPT understand Hausa?

      OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol scored 59 with Omnilingual and 59 with Intron, 3rd of 13 at best. Gemini 3.1 Pro with direct audio and Kimi K3 with Omnilingual scored higher.

      Is Omnilingual or Intron better for Hausa?

      Omnilingual scored higher with 4 of the 6 language models, and Intron with the other 2. Neither is better everywhere, so test your own model and audio. The biggest gap is Claude Sonnet 5, which scored 50 with Omnilingual and 39 with Intron.

      How did you test?

      We recorded 30 questions about pregnancy, childbirth and newborn care in Hausa, with reference answers drawn from the guideline “Essential obstetric and newborn care”. Each chain transcribed, translated and answered them, and every answer was scored against the reference answer by comparing meaning. Accuracy is the mean score over the 30 questions and response time is the median.

      Can I use this for medical advice?

      No. These scores measure how closely an answer matches a reference answer. They are not a clinical safety review, and even the best chain never scored above 80 on a single question. Have clinicians review any health assistant before it reaches patients.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI should my Hausa agent use?

Start with Gemini 3.1 Pro + built-in audio: it scored highest on real Hausa questions (71 out of 100) and answers in a median 16.3 s. You can switch chains later without rebuilding.

Can it work on a phone line?

Not yet. None of the 13 chains we tested answered in under 10 s, which calls need; the fastest takes 13.6 s (Claude Sonnet 5 + Omnilingual). Use WhatsApp or the web for now, and test a phone line before launch.

What does it cost to run?

On Gooey.AI the observed charge was $40.00 to $62.67 per 1,000 Hausa runs, depending on the chain. The calculator above scales it to your volume.

Can I keep data in Nigeria?

Yes, with a sovereign-deployable chain: Kimi K3 + Omnilingual, Kimi K3 + Intron, DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual and DeepSeek V4 + Intron can run in Nigeria or on your own servers. Other chains use a private API, which sends audio to its maker’s cloud. Hosting types are still being confirmed.

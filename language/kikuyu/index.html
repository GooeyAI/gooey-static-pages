    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best AI for Kikuyu?

    In our Aug 30, 2026 test, Gemini 3.1 Pro with Kikuyu Whisper v2 scored highest: 85.8 out of 100 with a 9.46-second median response. GPT-5.6 Sol + Kikuyu Whisper v2 scored 82.5, and Gemini 3.7 Flash + Kikuyu Whisper v2 scored 81.7.

    Which chain is best for phone calls?

    Gemini 3.7 Flash + Kikuyu Whisper v2 is the fastest chain scoring at least 80: 81.7 out of 100 in 7.06 seconds. Gemini 3.1 Pro + Kikuyu Whisper v2 is more accurate at 85.8 and still below the 10-second median target at 9.46 seconds.

    Does ChatGPT understand Kikuyu?

    OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol scored 82.5 with Kikuyu Whisper v2 and 67.5 with Omnilingual. In this benchmark, changing the speech-recognition step improved the score by 15 points.

    Can these Kikuyu AI chains run on local servers?

    Yes. The Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4 chains paired with Omnilingual or Kikuyu Whisper v2 are marked sovereign-deployable because their model steps can be hosted on infrastructure you control. The benchmark export does not identify the physical location used for each test run, so the label describes deployment capability rather than the benchmark’s execution location.

    How did you test?

    We used 24 general-knowledge questions about Kenya spoken in Kikuyu. Each chain listened, transcribed and answered, and its answer was scored against a reference answer by semantic similarity. Accuracy is the mean score over 24 questions; response time is the median.

      Frequently asked questions

      Can I run a Kinyarwanda AI on my own servers in Rwanda?

      Four chains can: DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3, each with Omnilingual or Intron speech recognition. Both models have open weights, Omnilingual is open, and Intron says Sahara v2 can run offline on local hardware. DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual also scored highest in our Aug 30, 2026 test (92 out of 100, 7.1 s). Every other chain needs a private API, which sends audio to its maker’s cloud.

      What is the best AI for Kinyarwanda?

      In our Aug 30, 2026 test, DeepSeek V4 with Omnilingual speech recognition scored highest (92 out of 100, 7.1 s). Gemini 3.1 Pro, which listens to the audio directly, came second (90, 8.9 s).

      Which is best for phone calls, and which for WhatsApp?

      For phone calls, GPT-5.6 Sol + Omnilingual is the fastest private chain scoring 85 or more (6.9 s) and DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual the fastest sovereign-deployable one (7.1 s, score 92). Kimi K3 + Omnilingual answered fastest of all (6.7 s) but scored 78. Ten of the 11 chains answer in under 10 s. For WhatsApp voice notes and text a second or two matters less: DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual is the most accurate (92) and Gemini 3.1 Pro with direct audio the best private chain (90).

      Does ChatGPT understand Kinyarwanda?

      OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol scored 85 with Omnilingual and 81 with Intron, 6th of 11 at best.

      How did you test?

      Native speakers recorded 24 health and farming questions as WhatsApp voice notes. Each chain transcribed, translated and answered them, and every answer was scored against an expert’s. Evaluated with CLEAR Global.

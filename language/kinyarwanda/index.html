      Frequently asked questions

      What is the best AI for Kinyarwanda?

      DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual was the highest-scoring chain in our 2026-08-30 Kinyarwanda benchmark. It achieved a Kinyarwanda Index of 92/100 with a median response time of 7.1 seconds across 24 questions; see the benchmark results. This result applies to the tested questions and workflows, not every use case.

      Can AI models understand and answer questions in Kinyarwanda?

      Yes—the tested workflows processed spoken Kinyarwanda questions and produced answers in English. Across 11 chains, the Kinyarwanda Index ranged from 68 to 92/100 in the benchmark results. These are complete-workflow results, not standalone speech-recognition scores or a test of answers written in Kinyarwanda.

      Can I run Kinyarwanda AI on my own servers?

      Open-weight language models such as DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3 offer a starting point for running Kinyarwanda AI on your own servers. A fully local voice workflow also requires speech recognition that supports Kinyarwanda on local hardware. Choose hardware that meets the models’ requirements and check each component’s licence and language support before deployment.

      Which Kinyarwanda AI models are best for phone calls and WhatsApp?

      For phone-call pilots, DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual is the highest-scoring chain below our 10-second median response-time target (92/100, 7.1 seconds). For WhatsApp voice-note replies, DeepSeek V4 + Omnilingual had the highest Kinyarwanda Index (92/100) in the benchmark results. These timings exclude a complete phone connection and spoken reply; test telephony, network and text-to-speech latency before launch.

      How much does a Kinyarwanda AI workflow cost?

      In our 2026-08-30 benchmark, the observed average whole-workflow Gooey.AI charge was $0.04 per question, or $40.00 per 1,000 questions, depending on the chain. The pricing snapshot calculates each chain’s average independently from its own recorded runs, including the speech-recognition or direct-audio step used by that workflow. These historical charges are not current provider list prices or a guarantee of future costs.

      What does the Kinyarwanda Index measure?

      The Kinyarwanda Index is the mean semantic-similarity score between a workflow’s answers and the reference answers, scaled from 0 to 100. The evaluation CSV records scores for the 24 benchmark questions, with a higher Index indicating closer agreement in meaning. It is not the percentage of questions answered correctly, a standalone transcription score or a safety certification.

      How were the Kinyarwanda AI models evaluated?

      On 2026-08-30, we evaluated 11 chains using 24 spoken Kinyarwanda questions: 22 general-knowledge questions about Kenya and two medical questions. The workflows used direct audio or speech recognition with a language model, and their answers were scored by semantic similarity against the reference answers supplied in the dataset; the evaluation CSV contains the questions, references, scores and run links. Scores are averaged and response times are medians; this small dataset does not establish performance across all topics, accents or real-world conditions. These benchmark results are not a clinical safety assessment. Any clinical or patient-facing use requires qualified clinical review.

      What is the environmental impact of Kinyarwanda AI models?

      Our EcoCost estimates cover text-model inference for 8 of the 11 tested Kinyarwanda chains, not their full environmental impact. The EcoCost snapshot reports estimated CO₂e per 1,000 questions and uncertainty ranges with low confidence. Speech recognition and direct-audio inference are excluded, and missing estimates are not zero; these data cannot establish the lowest-impact complete voice workflow.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Kinyarwanda speech to text?

Meta’s Omnilingual was the strongest speech recognition in our Aug 30, 2026 Kinyarwanda benchmark: chains using it averaged a Kinyarwanda Index of 84.6/100, against 81.4/100 for Intron. Gemini 3.1 Pro (direct audio), which needs no separate speech step, scored 90/100. See the benchmark results. These are complete-workflow results, not standalone speech-recognition scores.

Does it work on WhatsApp voice notes and phone calls?

The 24 benchmark questions were sent as WhatsApp voice notes. For phone-call pilots, 10 of the 11 chains stayed below our 10-second median response-time target; the fastest was Kimi K3 + Omnilingual (6.7 seconds). These timings exclude a complete phone connection and spoken reply; test telephony, network and text-to-speech latency before launch.

Can I run it on my own servers?

Omnilingual is an open-weight speech recognition model from Meta, so it can run on your own servers; paired with an open-weight language model such as DeepSeek V4, the whole Kinyarwanda chain can run on local hardware. Intron’s offline option still needs confirming for Kinyarwanda, and Gemini 3.1 Pro is a private API. Choose hardware that meets the models’ requirements and check each component’s licence and language support before deployment.

What does the Kinyarwanda Index measure?

The Kinyarwanda Index is the mean semantic-similarity score between a workflow’s answers and the reference answers, scaled from 0 to 100. The evaluation CSV records scores for the 24 benchmark questions, with a higher Index indicating closer agreement in meaning. It is not the percentage of questions answered correctly, a standalone transcription score or a safety certification.

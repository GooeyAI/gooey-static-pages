Gooey elements

Every element a page can use, with every slot filled. Pages contain content only: copy an example, change its text, and keep the slot names. Start new pages from page-skeleton.html.

gooey-page

Wraps the whole page body, exactly once. Until the shared module has loaded, its children are hidden behind a spinner. Attribute theme: light (default) or dark. 


        
      


      

        
gooey-header

        

          The fixed bar at the top of this page. Slots:
          title (fades in after scrolling), default (nav links,
          hidden on phones), login (keep it a real
          <a> whose text is exactly Login;
          gooey.ai swaps it for the user's name).
        

        

      


      

        
gooey-hero

        

          The first thing on every page, right after the header. Slots:
          eyebrow, title (the page's one
          h1), default (subtitle), cta (buttons),
          byline, image (beside the text on wide
          screens, below it on phones).

            A page title that can run to two lines
            A one-sentence subtitle that says what the page is about.

            Secondary action
            By Sean Blagsvedt & Archana Prasad · Founders, Gooey.AI
            
          
        

        

      


      

        
gooey-section

        

          A full-width band of content. Slots: heading
          (an h2), intro, default (paragraphs, lists,
          columns, images…). Attribute variant:
          plain (default), tinted, dark.
        

        

          
            Section heading
            An introductory sentence, a little larger and softer than body text.
            
Body paragraphs go straight into the section. Links and bold text work as usual.

            
A sub-heading

            
    
              
  • Lists are fine too.
    • 
              
  • Each item on its own line.
    • 
            

          
          
            A dark section
            
Use sparingly, for one standout band per page.

gooey-faq + gooey-faq-item

        

          Questions that open and close (also with the keyboard).
          gooey-faq-item slots: question, default
          (the answer).
        

        

          
            
              What is an AI workflow?
              
A human-readable recipe: prompts, model choices, tools, knowledge documents and evaluations bundled around a real use case.

            
            
              Can I fork one?
              
Yes. Browse workflows on gooey.ai/explore.
